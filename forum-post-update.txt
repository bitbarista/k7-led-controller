Update: PC Bridge app, Android app, mobile schedule chart editor, and more

The biggest additions since the last update are two new ways to control the lamp — one for desktop, one for Android.


PC Bridge (Windows & Linux)

The K7 Controller is no longer ESP32-only. The PC Bridge is a small program that runs on your Windows or Linux PC and talks directly to the lamp over WiFi. It serves the full controller UI in your browser — the same schedule editor, presets, profiles, and push that you get on the ESP32 version.

No hardware to buy. Download, run, connect to the lamp's WiFi, and you're in.

Windows: run the installer, double-click the desktop shortcut.
Linux: download the AppImage, make it executable, double-click.

Download links are on the setup guide (see below).


Android App

There's now a native Android app. Same idea as the PC Bridge — it runs a local bridge inside your phone and talks to the lamp directly over WiFi. Full schedule editing, community presets, profiles, backup/restore, and push. No ESP32 required.

Not on the Play Store yet (Google charges a publishing fee for a free open-source project). Sideloading takes about two minutes — the setup guide walks through it step by step.


Mobile schedule chart editor

Both the Android app and the mobile browser view now have a proper interactive schedule chart. Tap any hour bar and scroll to adjust brightness. The colour preview strip updates live so you can see the blended output across the day as you edit.

The trapezoidal generator and granular list editors are still there, but the chart is a much better starting point for most edits.


Community preset browser

All variants — ESP32, PC Bridge, and Android — now include a built-in browser for the community presets. Browse and apply any preset without leaving the app.


New app icon

A proper icon across all variants: launcher, taskbar, Windows exe, and installer. Small thing but it makes it feel a lot more finished.


Download page

The setup guide now has a downloads section at the top — buttons for Windows, Linux, and Android that always link to the current release automatically. No hunting through GitHub.


What works on PC/Android

Full schedule chart editor, all built-in presets, community preset browser, profiles, backup/restore, push to lamp.


What still needs the ESP32

Smooth Ramp, Feed mode, Maintenance mode, Acclimation, Seasonal Shift — anything that needs to keep running while you're not connected. The ESP32 version isn't going anywhere.


A note on Android and iOS availability

The Android app isn't on the Play Store because Google charges a registration fee to publish there, and this is a free open-source project. There's no iOS version for the same reason: Apple requires an annual developer fee. If either changes in future I'll look at it, but Android sideloading is straightforward and the guide covers it.
