Public Profiles

Built-in and reviewed community profiles contain only the lamp model, schedule, manual values, and notes. Download one, then use Import Preset in the controller UI, or show an app-compatible QR code for users who only want to use the lamp app.

Profile notes are guidance, not guarantees. Coral suitability and PAR/umol estimates are approximate and may not match your tank. Actual output depends on lamp version, mounting height, tank depth, water clarity, optics, flow, rockwork, and how measurements were taken. Start conservatively, observe livestock, and adjust to your own system.
